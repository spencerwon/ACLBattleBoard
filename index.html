How scoring works

❤️ Love scores 8.1–10.0, 👍 Like 6.1–8.0, 😐 Meh 4.1–6.0 and 👎 Nope 0.1–4.0. Your rating picks the band. Head-to-head battles then set the order inside the band: each newly rated artist battles members of its band (a binary search, so a band of 15 takes about 4 battles) until its spot is clear. Scores are spread across the band on a bell curve, so the first ❤️ sits at 9.0, and a second ❤️ that beats it lands at 9.5 while the first drops to 8.5. As a band fills up, neighbours can share a score, but the numbered order never ties.

With head-to-head placement switched off, a newly rated artist sits at the middle of its band at the band's centre score until you battle it.

A lower-rated act can beat a higher-rated one in a set-clash battle without changing bands, because genre and mood don't always line up. You choose whether to move either act or keep both ratings.
