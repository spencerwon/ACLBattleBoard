How scoring works

❤️ Love scores 8.1–10.0, 👍 Like 6.1–8.0, 😐 Meh 4.1–6.0 and 👎 Nope 0.1–4.0. Your rating picks the band. Once you've rated everyone in view, head-to-head battles set the order inside each band. Newly rated artists take turns, one battle each, so you don't face the same act over and over, and each needs only about 4 battles to find its spot in a band of 15 (a binary search). Cards come broad first: every genre early, best-known names first. “Not sure yet” sends an artist to the back of the line, behind every new artist and battle; skip it twice and it waits until the very end. Filters limit the line to the artists in view. Scores are spread across the band on a bell curve, so the first ❤️ sits at 9.0, and a second ❤️ that beats it lands at 9.5 while the first drops to 8.5. As a band fills up, neighbours can share a score, but the numbered order never ties.

With head-to-head placement switched off, a newly rated artist sits at the middle of its band at the band's centre score until you battle it.

A lower-rated act can beat a higher-rated one in a set-clash battle without changing bands, because genre and mood don't always line up. You choose whether to move either act or keep both ratings.
